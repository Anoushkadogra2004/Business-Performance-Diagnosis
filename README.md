# Business Performance Diagnosis

## Project Overview
A data analytics project analyzing 3,000 sales transactions from 2025 to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, regional performance, returns, and delivery efficiency.

## Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning and preparation
- **MySQL:** Data querying and business analysis
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and visualization

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Orders:** 3,000
- **Unique Customers:** 598
- **Total Sales:** ₹3.54 Crore
- **Reported Net Profit:** ₹40.98 Lakh
- **Average Order Value:** ₹11,813
- **Average Customer Rating:** 4.11/5
- **Return Rate:** 9.63%
- **Delayed Orders:** 701 (23.37%)

## Key Business Insights
- **Product Category:** Electronics recorded the highest category sales at approximately ₹1.10 crore.
- **Regional Performance:** North recorded the highest sales among the four regions.
- **Sales Channel:** Website generated the highest sales and profit among the three channels.
- **Monthly Performance:** August recorded the highest monthly sales, while June recorded the lowest.
- **Returns:** 289 orders were returned, representing 9.63% of all orders.
- **Delivery:** 23.37% of orders were delayed.
- **Discount Analysis:** Average net profit per order was higher for orders without discounts than for discounted orders.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate delivery delays by region and delivery duration.
2. Analyze product return patterns and common return reasons.
3. Evaluate discount strategies using average profit per order and profit margin.
4. Identify growth opportunities in high-performing products and regions.
5. Monitor sales, profitability, returns, and delivery performance through the Power BI dashboard.
